Delivery model

Discover. Design. Build.

Every engagement runs through the same three gated phases, with one pod that stays with you the whole way.

Phase 1 — 2 to 4 weeks

Discover

We qualify the opportunity against strategic intent, risk posture, and expected economic value, and rule out anything that isn't defensible before real investment starts.

Phase 2 — 4 to 8 weeks

Design

Validated ideas become production-ready blueprints: architecture, decision ownership, governance controls, and the success metrics we'll build toward.

Phase 3 — ongoing

Build

We prototype and ship a production-grade system, generate real adoption evidence, and scale it only once the value clearly outweighs the cost and risk.
